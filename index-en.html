Frequently asked questions

Do you offer an airport shuttle?

Yes. Both addresses are a few minutes from Cotonou airport. Give your flight number when you book and a driver will meet you on arrival.

How does payment work?

No online payment: reception confirms your request and tells you the deposit, payable by Mobile Money, card, transfer or cash. The balance is paid on site.

Can I cancel or change my dates?

At Amazing Grace Residence Luxury, cancelling or changing dates is free up to 24 h before arrival, from the Manage my booking page.

Is breakfast included?

Yes at Amazing Grace Residence Luxury, in every night’s rate. At Fidjrossè, reception will let you know when you book.
