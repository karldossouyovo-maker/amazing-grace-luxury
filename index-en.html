A boutique hotel in Cotonou, between the airport and the beach

Amazing Grace Luxury Résidence is an upscale hotel in Cotonou, Benin, five minutes from the airport and steps from the beach. Nine rooms, studios, apartments and suites, each named after a precious stone.

Breakfast is included every morning. Reception is open 24 hours a day, rooms are cleaned daily, and the airport shuttle, parking, restaurant and bar are on site.

Whether you are coming home for the holidays, travelling for work or discovering Benin, book direct: reception replies by email or WhatsApp.
