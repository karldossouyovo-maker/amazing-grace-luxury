FAQ

Before you come

Is breakfast included?

Yes, it is included every morning in every room rate.

How do I get here from the airport?

The residence is five minutes by road. An airport shuttle is available: add your flight number to your request.

How does booking work?

You send your request on the site, with nothing to pay. Reception confirms availability and the deposit by email or WhatsApp; the room is held once the deposit arrives.

How can I pay from abroad?

By card, bank transfer or Mobile Money. On site, cash and cheques are also accepted.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is free for guests.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed at the residence.

Is the residence wheelchair accessible?

Unfortunately not: the building is not adapted for guests with reduced mobility.

Can I give a stay as a gift?

Yes, with our gift vouchers: an amount or nights in the room of your choice.
